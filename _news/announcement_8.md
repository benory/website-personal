I presented “Programming the Past: Belgium and the Early Music Performance Tradition” at this year's [Script and Sound Seminar](https://www.kbr.be/en/agenda/script-and-sound-seminar-2026/). Drawing on more than 500 concert programs from 1915–1960, I traced how a varied repertoire shaped by regional and national preferences increasingly gave way to a shared international canon after 1945. Belgian programs reveal a particularly strong commitment to Monteverdi, anticipating his later prominence in early music performance and scholarship. Thanks to Antonio Chemotti and the Royal Library of Belgium for such an engaging program.
