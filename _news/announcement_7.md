I presented my latest research, “When Did Renaissance Musicians Cross the Alps?” at the [Second International Digital Musicology Conference](https://idmc-2026.nifc.pl/#schedule) in Kraków. Drawing on [Mapping the Musical Renaissance](https://renaissancemapping.org), my project explores how a better understanding of mobility can help test accounts of journeys by musicians and composers, including Josquin des Prez and Jacob Obrecht. The Fryderyk Chopin Institute was a wonderfully generous host—it was a pleasure to exchange ideas with colleagues and make new connections with others working in digital scholarship.
